The short version

  • Your card photos stay on your phone. Grading math (centering, photo checks) runs on your phone.
  • We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and we don't sell or share data for advertising.
  • If you turn on "Improve grading with my card photos," we receive cropped photos of the card only, plus its grade, with nothing that identifies you.
  • You can delete what we hold about you at any time in Settings → Delete My Data.

What stays on your phone

Card photos, your collection, what you paid, submissions you log, and your settings are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app or using Delete My Data removes them.

What we receive

1. An anonymous sign-in (only if you use alerts or photo sharing)

A random code that lets our server send price alerts to your device. We store a one-way hash of it, the date it was created, an under-13 flag, the cards you starred, and your push-notification address if you allowed notifications. It isn't linked to your name, email or any other account.

2. Card lookups

To show prices and outcomes, the app sends our server the card's catalog ID or the text it read off the card (name and number). Card photos are not sent for this.

3. Slab results you add

When you add a slab result, we receive the grader, the grade, the tier, the card's catalog ID, our estimate, measurements such as centering ratios and photo-quality scores, and optional dates and sale price. We store these without any user ID, device ID, IP address or exact time, so we cannot link them back to you — which also means we can't find them to delete them later.

4. Training photos (optional, 13 and older only)

If "Improve grading with my card photos" is on, after you add a slab result we receive cropped photos of the card's front and back. The crop removes anything around the card. Photos are re-encoded on your phone first, which removes camera data such as location, phone model and the time the photo was taken. Cards with writing or signatures on the border are skipped. We use these photos only to train and test GemReady's grading model.

5. Feedback

If you send feedback, we receive what you write, the type of feedback, the screen you came from and the app version. Please don't include personal information.

6. Service logs

Our hosting provider [HOSTING PROVIDER] may keep standard request logs (such as IP addresses) for security for up to [N] days. We don't use them to identify users.

Children

We ask everyone for their birth year when the app first opens. For people under 13, the app never creates the anonymous sign-in, and photo sharing, price alerts and free-text feedback are turned off. The age you enter stays on your phone.

Service providers

We use [HOSTING PROVIDER] to run our server and database. Price data comes from TCGplayer (via tcgcsv.com) and [PRICECHARTING, if enabled]; the app sends them nothing about you. Text recognition runs on your phone; the recognition library is downloaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net the first time it's used.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn photo sharing off in Settings → Privacy at any time.
  • Turn price alerts off in Settings → Watchlist.
  • Settings → Delete My Data deletes your anonymous sign-in, alerts and push address from our servers right away, and everything stored on your phone.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Because we don't hold data that identifies you, Delete My Data covers everything we can link to you. Contact [CONTACT EMAIL] with questions.

Security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Anonymous sign-ins and alerts are kept until you delete them or [N] months without use. Anonymous slab results and training photos are kept while they help improve grading.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this policy and show the privacy notice in the app again before the change applies to you.
